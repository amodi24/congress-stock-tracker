Senate data: efdsearch.senate.gov. House data: the Clerk of the House's financial disclosure site. Insider data: SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings. This is an independent, non-commercial project for personal informational use — not affiliated with the U.S. Senate, the U.S. House, or the SEC.

Amounts are disclosed as ranges, not exact figures, per STOCK Act rules (insider amounts are the exception: exact shares × price from Form 4). Insider coverage is limited to non-derivative (direct stock) transactions at companies senators have traded, not the full market. House reports are filed as PDFs; a small share (roughly one in eight) are older scanned documents with no extractable text and are excluded rather than guessed at.
