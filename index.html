Senate data: efdsearch.senate.gov, the Senate's official electronic financial disclosure system. Insider data: SEC EDGAR Form 4 filings. This is an independent, non-commercial project for personal informational use — not affiliated with the U.S. Senate or the SEC.

Senate amounts are disclosed as ranges, not exact figures, per STOCK Act rules; insider amounts are exact (shares × price) from Form 4. Insider coverage is limited to non-derivative (direct stock) transactions at companies senators have traded, not the full market, and House of Representatives trades are not yet included.
